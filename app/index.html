Room settings

Bring to this room:

Background blur

Blur is on the first time you turn the camera on. It is a guess about where you end and the room begins, so edges, low light and quick movement all leak pieces of the room for a frame at a time. It costs battery, and on a slow machine it costs frame rate. It makes a room harder to read. It is not a guarantee that nobody can see it.

Voice off

This is voice masking, not anonymity. It shifts your pitch and moves the resonances of your voice separately from it, which is enough that someone who knows your voice will not place it straight away. It will not stop anyone holding a recording of you who wants to match it, and it does not survive a forensic comparison. Do not lean on it where being identified would matter.

Off: nothing that says it is an agent is sent your camera or microphone. It never leaves this device for them.

Room's open. Send the link.

Anyone who receives or is forwarded this link can enter while it is current. The link is an invitation, not the room's encryption key, and it stays in the URL fragment so the web server and link preview fetcher do not receive it. The messaging service carrying the link can still read it unless that conversation is end-to-end encrypted.

Keep the browser that opened the room online: it answers new arrivals without showing you a prompt.

Rotating stops the old link admitting anybody new. It does not remove somebody who already entered.

What carrying costs you

Two people who cannot reach each other directly are a pair. This device will carry three pairs at most, and stops being asked once it is full.

You would be carrying people already in the room with you. You can see and hear them now, and passing their video on gives you nothing you did not already have. What it does not let you do is pass one person off as another: every stream is tagged to whoever sent it, and a relabelled one fails on arrival.

Turn it off whenever you like, including mid-call. Anyone you were carrying falls back to another route on their own, and neither your room nor theirs ends.
