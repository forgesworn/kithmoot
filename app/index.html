    Room settings

    Bring to this room:

    Background blur

    Blur is on the first time you turn the camera on. It is a guess about where you end and the room begins, so edges, low light and quick movement all leak pieces of the room for a frame at a time. It costs battery, and on a slow machine it costs frame rate. It makes a room harder to read. It is not a guarantee that nobody can see it.

    Voice off

    This is voice masking, not anonymity. It shifts your pitch and moves the resonances of your voice separately from it, which is enough that someone who knows your voice will not place it straight away. It will not stop anyone holding a recording of you who wants to match it, and it does not survive a forensic comparison. Do not lean on it where being identified would matter. It does not change your accent, your words or the way you phrase them, and anyone who knows those will still know you.

    Off: nothing that says it is an agent is sent your camera or microphone. It never leaves this device for them.

    Room's open. Send the link.

    Anyone who receives or is forwarded this link can enter while it is current. The link is an invitation, not the room's encryption key, and it stays in the URL fragment so the web server and link preview fetcher do not receive it. The messaging service carrying the link can still read it unless that conversation is end-to-end encrypted.

    Keep the browser that opened the room online: it answers new arrivals without showing you a prompt.

    Rotating stops the old link admitting anybody new. It does not remove somebody who already entered.

    What carrying costs you

    Two people who cannot reach each other directly are a pair. This device will carry three pairs at most, and stops being asked once it is full.

    You would be carrying people already in the room with you. You can see and hear them now, and passing their video on gives you nothing you did not already have. What it does not let you do is pass one person off as another: every stream is tagged to whoever sent it, and a relabelled one fails on arrival.

    Turn it off whenever you like, including mid-call. Anyone you were carrying falls back to another route on their own, and neither your room nor theirs ends.

    Chat

    Drop a file on the chat, or choose one. It is encrypted on this device under a fresh key before anything leaves it, the encrypted bytes go to the Blossom server named below, and the key goes into the message, encrypted to the room like the words around it. The server sees an encrypted blob and this device's key; the relays see an announcement that this device shared some encrypted bytes. Nobody fetches anything until they choose to.

    Or a file already shared through Wildbloom: paste its file event (the id Wildbloom showed you, or the signed event JSON) and the recovery key.

    Host

    You are on this room’s admin list. Remove moves the room to a new key that person is not given: they can read nothing from here on, and what they already read stays theirs. Mute is a request their own client honours by stopping its camera and microphone; nothing in the middle can force it. Close ends the room for everybody.

    Transcript

    What people said aloud, written down by an agent that was allowed to listen. Nothing appears here from anybody who has Agents can hear me off. Who said what is the transcriber's claim, shown beside a key like any name.

    Minutes

    What a call came to, written by a scribe in the room from the transcript: who was here, what was decided, who took what on, what is still open. Type !minutes in the chat to have them written now; a scribe also writes them when the call ends. Nothing from anybody with Agents can hear me off is in them, and like a transcript they are the scribe's claim.
