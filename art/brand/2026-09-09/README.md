# Kithmoot identity assets

Approved 9 September 2026. `artwork.png` is the primary textured image for
marketing, ordinary app icons and product entrances. Keep it unchanged as the
source master. `prompt.txt` records the exact built-in image generation prompt.
`icon.svg` and `mono.svg` are construction and monochrome system companions;
they do not replace the textured artwork on ordinary app icons.

The product images are included in the repository. Native Android launchers use
the original PNG with an adaptive wrapper; themed system icons use a monochrome
vector. Deployment and store publication remain separate release steps.
