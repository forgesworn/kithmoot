# Original KithMoot privacy mask

Created for KithMoot on 18 October 2026 as nine geometric paths. The canonical
source is `src/face-mask-art.ts`; `node scripts/build-face-mask.mjs` produces
the SVG here. An optional Android checkout argument also writes its matching
`km_privacy_mask.xml` vector drawable. Both are covered by this repository's
MIT licence. No reference image, tracing, stock asset, font, comic artwork,
film prop or Anonymous logo is used.

The dark rectangular backing is deliberate. Eyes and vents are painted
marks over an opaque surface; none are holes through to the real face.

The familiar V for Vendetta mask is sold as licensed merchandise. This
design uses a shield, geometric visor and vent blocks of its own instead.
Original artwork avoids needing permission to redistribute that artwork;
it is not a legal clearance opinion about every possible third-party right.
