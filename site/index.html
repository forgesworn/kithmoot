Everything a group needs in one room.

Messages, files and people stay put between calls, so a room is somewhere you come back to, not a meeting link you throw away.

Rooms that stay open

A room keeps its messages, files and members. It's a group by default, so anyone with the link can come back even when nobody else is online.

Calls and screen sharing

Voice, video and screen sharing from the room you're already in. In the desktop app, share just one area, black out parts of it, and draw on it together.

Threads, edits and mentions

Reply in a thread, fix a typo, take a message back. Type @ and a name to reach that person on every device they use.

Files, encrypted first

Files are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded. Opening a conversation doesn't download them.

Private conversations

Start a one-to-one from anyone's name. Nobody else can ever be let in, and calls and files work just as they do in a group.

AI agents as members

Invite an agent with the same link as a person. It's labelled as an agent, shows whose it is, and hears your microphone only if you switch that on.

Phone and laptop as one

Pair your devices and show up as one person: the camera from your phone, the screen share from your laptop, one microphone.

Your rooms follow you

Sign in with Nostr and your rooms, and where you've read up to, follow you between devices. Search by words, person or file name.

What's private, and what isn't.

KithMoot runs over Nostr relays that anyone can operate. A room names its own relays, none of them holds the guest list, and the code is open to read.

How it works, in detail

Relays can't read your room

Messages, names and files are encrypted on your device before they leave it. Relays still see device keys, opaque room identifiers, timing and traffic volume.

The link is the key

Whoever has the full invitation can ask to join, so send it somewhere private. Changing the invitation stops new joins; it doesn't remove people already in.

Camera and microphone start off

Agents hear neither until you allow it. Profile lookups and notifications are optional. Background blur can miss edges, and a failed effect can show your unblurred camera.

Questions.

Found a bug? Use the bug-report tool in Room details, or open an issue on GitHub. Something unsafe or illegal? Use the report page.

Can this replace our team chat?

That's the aim. Not there yet: a conversation loads the last 30 days and 500 messages, and search covers only what's loaded. Channels, roles and removing people need a keeper (below). The wire format isn't frozen, so expect an update along the way. The changelog lists what has shipped.

Do I need a Nostr account?

No. Follow an invitation and pick a name. If you already have a Nostr key, sign in with your signer and your rooms follow you across devices, as long as the signer supports encryption.

Why won't my invitation open?

It may have expired or been replaced, or nobody may be online to answer it. Ask the person who sent it to open the room and send you a current link.

Does the room stay open when I leave?

A group does: it lets the next person in from the link with nobody online. A temporary meeting needs a member online to answer its link.

Can I delete a message?

You can take back or correct one you sent, and everyone sees it marked. It isn't erased: devices that received it keep their copy, and relays keep the encrypted event.

Can I remove somebody?

In a room run by a keeper, yes. A keeper is a small program that runs on your own computer or server; it can name hosts who remove members, and removal changes the room key. It can't erase what somebody already received.

Can I use an iPhone or iPad?

Yes, in the browser, for chat and calls. Screen sharing from iOS isn't supported, and there's no native iOS app yet.

What are the current limits?

Chat loads up to 500 messages from the last 30 days. Large calls depend on bandwidth and haven't been tested at every room size. Android shows threads, edits and retractions but sends plain messages.
