macOS · preview

Apple Silicon

For M1, M2, M3, M4 and later Apple Silicon Macs. Requires macOS 13 or later. No Intel Mac build yet.

Not notarised. This preview is ad-hoc signed, without a Developer ID signature. macOS may block it when downloaded.

Download for Mac

Version 0.1.9 · 131 MiB · SHA-256 checksums

Install on macOS
  1. Download and open the ZIP.
  2. Quit any running copy of KithMoot, then move KithMoot.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open KithMoot. If blocked, review Apple's guidance for opening an unnotarised app. Only approve it if you trust this download.

Updates are manual: quit the app and replace it with the next version. Your desktop login is separate from your browser login.

Linux · preview

Choose your processor

Intel or AMD laptop or desktop? Choose x64. An ARM64 computer? Choose ARM64. Run uname -m if unsure: x86_64 means x64; aarch64 means ARM64.

Download Linux x64

Version 0.1.9 · 125 MiB · SHA-256 checksums

Download Linux ARM64

Version 0.1.9 · 126 MiB · SHA-256 checksums

Install on Linux
  1. Extract the downloaded tar.gz archive.
  2. Open a terminal in the extracted KithMoot folder.
  3. Quit KithMoot if it is running, then run python3 install.py.
  4. Open KithMoot from your applications menu.

The installer runs as your own user, without sudo. It installs under your user data directory and adds a menu entry. Run the new version's installer to update; your profile is preserved.

Requires Python 3, a current glibc-based desktop with GTK 3, NSS and ALSA libraries, and permission to use unprivileged user namespaces. Some distributions restrict these; ask your administrator if the sandbox prevents startup.

ARM64 has passed an install and launch test in a Debian container. The x64 archive is built but has not yet been run. Physical GNOME, KDE and Wayland checks remain outstanding.

Android · 0.6.7

Your chats and rooms on your phone.

Requires Android 13 or later. This release makes rejoining a room work first time instead of needing a few goes, tells the rest of the room when you are on a call so their Join call button appears, turns with the phone all four ways (your rotation lock is respected), and removes Google's usage reporting from background effects and the QR scanner. It keeps the fix for “I can see them but not hear them” from 0.6.6. It is about calls: tiles survive a camera or microphone toggle, muting keeps the microphone ready so unmuting is instant, tiles show who is muted and who you have silenced, and Backdrop puts a tropical sea behind you. It includes synced account conversations, project room links, profile and relay settings, an explicit Leave call control, and joined-room notifications with an optional Zen bell while connected. The download is larger than 0.6.4 because of the background feature.

Download and open the APK, then choose Update. Do not uninstall first. Its signing lineage preserves compatibility with the old website preview; sign in with the same Nostr account and use Chats to find your synced rooms. Browser-only rooms must first be added to your account. This restores access to available history, not a complete archive.

Production-key signed; direct download, not a Play Store listing. Android 8–12 cannot install this release. The older preview remains available below but lacks the sync and call improvements.

Desktop preview 0.1.9

Share your screen. Keep your sound where you chose.

Screen sharing keeps your chosen listening device. Drawings appear as you draw, images open at full size, and the Mac preview adds a resizable sharing area.

Download checksums · Release metadata

What's in this release?
  • Starting or stopping a screen share keeps sound on the device you chose. Listen here also works with your microphone and camera off.
  • Drawings are sent while you move your finger or mouse, rather than waiting until you lift it. Network delay still applies.
  • On Mac, Share an area opens a floating frame. Move it, resize it with the top-right handle, then press Start sharing. With Draw off you can work with the apps underneath; with Draw on you can mark the shared area alongside other people.
  • Click an image attachment to expand it, view its actual size or pop it out into another window.
  • Desktop capture requests shared sound and includes the Mac audio permission description. Available sound sources still depend on your operating system and permissions.
  • Returning to Call from Chat on a phone keeps the call running.
  • Earlier in 0.1.8: background blur and replacement no longer report usage statistics to Google. The camera effects library we use had started sending them; we moved to a version without it, and the build now fails if it ever comes back.
  • If a background effect stops working, the people you are talking to see only your backdrop, or a full blur, never your room. A notice beside your picture says so, and the effect retries by itself.
  • A plain, even-coloured shirt is no longer partly replaced by the backdrop.
  • A phone sharing its screen upright is shown whole: the name placeholder for a person without a camera sits beside a share instead of on top of it.
  • A screen-sharing error clears once sharing works, and on a Mac it says how to fix the Screen Recording permission instead of “Invalid capture constraints”.
  • Earlier in 0.1.7: The cause of “I can see them but not hear them” is fixed: two ends of a call could settle on different arrangements without either noticing, and now they notice and put it right.
  • A call with every camera off is a small strip with names and controls, and chat keeps the window.
  • A person’s name no longer flashes as their joining name first, and a plain word is only highlighted as a mention when the sender actually mentioned that person. The @ picker inserts the name you can see.
  • Leave call is red wherever it appears.
  • Chat takes the whole window when nothing is beside it, and only becomes a side drawer while a call is showing something.
  • With nobody on a call the call area is one line, not an empty panel. The button says Start call, Join call or Leave call, whichever is true.
  • Leaving a call no longer flashes the join screen.
  • The projects list on the left can be hidden and shown.
  • 💯 is among the reactions.
  • Calls recover by themselves when a signal or a relay connection is lost, instead of leaving one direction silent.
  • A person who muted themselves shows “muted” on their tile. Someone you silenced shows “silenced for you”.
  • When two people share, each sits beside their own screen and both stay in view. Chat slides in and out.
  • “Pop out” on a shared screen now opens its own window.
  • Sea backgrounds, with fish, under “Hide what is behind you”.
  • Leaving and tidying a room removes your read position too.
When will I receive notifications?

Enable notifications in KithMoot and allow them in your operating system. Keep the app window open or minimised. Quitting stops delivery; closing the last Linux window quits the app. Quiet rooms only receive notifications while that room is open.

Notifications name the sender and room. Message text is opt-in. Linux icon badges depend on your launcher; some desktops need an extension. The Zen bell is app audio: mute it using its own switch when you want silence, including during an OS Focus mode.

What still needs testing?

The new Mac sharing area still needs physical checks for capture, resizing, drawing alignment, system sound and echo. It must stay on one monitor; moving outside that monitor blanks the share. The floating area is not offered in the browser or Linux preview. Android outgoing device audio and native image viewing, and iPhone screen sharing, remain separate unfinished work. Earlier Mac previews have been installed on M1 and M4. Mac notification delivery, permission prompts and Dock badges still need physical acceptance. Linux ARM64 was exercised in a container; x64 and real desktop environments need further testing. macOS Developer ID signing and notarisation are still to come. There is no automatic updater.

How do I check my download?

Download SHA256SUMS.txt into the same folder as your archive. On macOS run shasum -a 256 KithMoot-0.1.9-mac-arm64.zip; on Linux run sha256sum KithMoot-0.1.9-linux-*.tar.gz. Compare the result with the matching filename in the checksums file.
