Install on macOS
  1. Download and open the ZIP.
  2. Quit any running copy of KithMoot, then move KithMoot.app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open KithMoot and approve the usual first-launch prompt. You may need to grant screen-recording permission again when upgrading from an older preview.

Signed Mac releases download quietly and wait until you approve a restart; calls, shares, uploads and unsent work block that restart. Your desktop login is separate from your browser login.

Linux · preview

Choose your processor

Intel or AMD laptop or desktop? Choose x64. An ARM64 computer? Choose ARM64. Run uname -m if unsure: x86_64 means x64; aarch64 means ARM64.

Install on Linux
  1. Extract the downloaded tar.gz archive.
  2. Open a terminal in the extracted KithMoot folder.
  3. Quit KithMoot if it is running, then run python3 install.py.
  4. Open KithMoot from your applications menu.

The installer runs as your own user, without sudo. It installs under your user data directory and adds a menu entry. Run the new version's installer to update; your profile is preserved.

Requires Python 3, a current glibc-based desktop with GTK 3, NSS and ALSA libraries, and permission to use unprivileged user namespaces. Some distributions restrict these; ask your administrator if the sandbox prevents startup.

Share Area works on X11 with the movable crop frame, and on Wayland through a preview: choose a screen, drag a box around what to share, and only that box is sent. Linux system audio is not captured. The Wayland screen chooser and physical X11, GNOME and KDE checks remain outstanding.

Windows · portable preview

Android · 0.6.12

Your chats and rooms on your phone.

Requires Android 13 or later. Version 0.6.12 keeps your call going while you visit other rooms: on a call, the back arrow now shows your rooms under a small bar with the microphone, Back to the call and Leave call, and any room you open from there is for reading and writing while the call carries on. Leave room still leaves.

It keeps the 0.6.11 fix for a crash: changing the camera, microphone or a share while the room was in a secure update, which is every link rotation, removal and rejoin, could end the app outright. It also fits the camera to the size of the call: one other person gets 720p, two or three get 540p, four or more get 360p, each with a ceiling on how much is sent, so a phone on a group video call runs cooler and uses less of your connection. Screen sharing is unchanged.

It keeps the 0.6.10 fix for the freeze in which KithMoot stopped responding during a call, and the admission proof for rejoining a kept room after a removal, which the room keepers now require; a phone on 0.6.9 or earlier that misses a removal cannot catch up until it updates.

It keeps the 0.6.9 repairs for calls that stayed on “Connecting video…”, and the 0.6.8 additions: permitted app playback when sharing your screen, separate microphone controls, deliberate Listen here device selection, live drawing and encrypted PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF viewing. Apps can block playback capture, and voice-call audio is excluded. Attachment uploads and a floating drawing area over other Android apps are not included.

An in-place upgrade from 0.6.10 to 0.6.11 has passed on a Pixel 10 Pro XL: the production signature and lineage verified, the app launched, and the existing account and room list remained. A live multi-person video call on this build, and how warm the phone gets on it, are the checks that remain.

It keeps synced account conversations, project room links, profile and relay settings, an explicit Leave call control, rotation support, and joined-room notifications with an optional Zen bell while connected.

Download and open the APK, then choose Update. Do not uninstall first. Its signing lineage preserves compatibility with the old website preview; sign in with the same Nostr account and use Chats to find your synced rooms. Browser-only rooms must first be added to your account. This restores access to available history, not a complete archive.

Production-key signed; direct download, not a Play Store listing. Android 8–12 cannot install this release. The older preview remains available below but lacks the sync and call improvements.

Desktop preview 0.1.18

Calls that reach you.

Mac, Linux and Windows are all on 0.1.18. The Mac build is signed and notarised, and Mac 0.1.14 updates itself.

When will I receive notifications?

Enable notifications in KithMoot and allow them in your operating system. Keep the app window open or minimised. Quitting stops delivery; closing the last Linux window quits the app. Quiet rooms only receive notifications while that room is open.

Notifications name the sender and room. Message text is opt-in. Linux icon badges depend on your launcher; some desktops need an extension. The Zen bell is app audio: mute it using its own switch when you want silence, including during an OS Focus mode.

What still needs testing?

The sharing area still needs physical checks for capture, resizing and drawing alignment. Linux X11 has not yet had a physical desktop run, and on Wayland nobody has yet checked that the system screen chooser appears once and that the far end sees the picture. Linux system audio is not captured. Android 0.6.12 has passed a production-signed in-place upgrade and launch on a Pixel 10 Pro XL, and its call dock was checked on that phone against a web peer before release; a live multi-person video call on it, and how warm the phone gets on one, remain pending, as do the existing iPhone and Mac physical checks.

How do I check my download?

Download SHA256SUMS.txt into the same folder as your archive. On macOS run shasum -a 256 KithMoot-0.1.18-mac-arm64.zip; on Linux run sha256sum KithMoot-0.1.18-linux-*.tar.gz; on Windows run Get-FileHash KithMoot-0.1.18-windows-x64.zip -Algorithm SHA256. Compare the result with the matching filename in the checksums file.
